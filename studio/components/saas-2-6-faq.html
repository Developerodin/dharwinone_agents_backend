FAQ

Questions from real support threads

Does forking touch my production database?

No. Forks are taken from streamed WAL snapshots, never from the primary. The heaviest thing we ever do to production is a read replica connection you configure once.

How does copy-on-write billing actually work?

You pay for the pages a branch changes, not the snapshot size. A typical feature branch against a 400 GB primary carries 200 to 800 MB of delta. The dashboard shows the exact figure per branch.

Can masking rules break foreign keys?

No. Masking is deterministic per value, so the same email always masks to the same token. Joins, uniqueness constraints, and foreign keys survive intact.

What Postgres versions are supported?

14 through 17, plus common extensions including PostGIS, pgvector, and pg_partman. MySQL support is in private beta behind a waitlist.

What happens if I hit the free tier limits?

Nothing breaks. New forks queue until a branch is reaped or you upgrade. We never silently delete a branch you are still using.
