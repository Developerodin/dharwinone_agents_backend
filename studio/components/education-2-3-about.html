Chapter I · Programmes of study

Three schools, taught in seminar

School of Letters

B.A. (Hons) Literature and History

Close reading across four literatures, a history sequence from antiquity to independence, and a senior thesis defended aloud.

  • Seminar size14 students
  • Duration3 years + honours year
  • Intake48 seats
School of Sciences

B.Sc. (Hons) Mathematics and Physics

Proof-first mathematics with a laboratory physics core. Every student completes a supervised research project by third year.

  • Seminar size16 students
  • Duration3 years + honours year
  • Intake52 seats
School of Economics

B.A. (Hons) Economics and Statistics

Theory taught against Indian data. Students run a field study in second year and publish it in the institute's working paper series.

  • Seminar size15 students
  • Duration3 years + honours year
  • Intake44 seats
