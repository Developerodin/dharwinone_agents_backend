Patient FAQ

Things patients ask at the front desk

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are seen in order of arrival during OPD hours. Booking a slot by phone holds your place in the queue, useful for evening OPD which is busiest between 6 and 8 pm.
How do I collect my lab reports?
Reports are sent as a PDF to the mobile number you register. Printed copies can be collected from reception after 6 pm the same day, or the next morning for samples given after 11 am.
Is a follow-up visit charged?
A review with the same doctor within 7 days of your consultation is free. Bring your prescription slip, it carries your visit number.
Which insurance policies do you accept?
We are empanelled with major insurers and TPAs for cashless OPD packages. For reimbursement claims, the front desk provides itemised bills and stamped reports on request, at no charge.
What should I bring for a first visit?
Any previous prescriptions, recent reports, and a list of medicines you take daily. If you track sugar or blood pressure at home, bring those readings too.
