Course syllabi

What each course actually covers

Class 10 Board Booster
Full CBSE and state board syllabus for Maths and Science, finished by December so the last three months are pure revision.
  • Chapter-wise tests every fortnight, 3 full pre-boards in January
  • Handwritten-style notes for every chapter, no photocopied guides
  • Board answer-writing workshop: how to earn stepwise marks
Class 12 PCM Intensive
Boards and JEE Main prepared together, with clearly separated problem sets so students always know which level they are practising.
  • Physics and Maths daily practice sheets, 20 problems each
  • Previous 10 years of board and JEE Main papers, solved in class
  • College counselling session for parents in February
NEET Repeater Batch
A full-day programme for students taking a second attempt, built around fixing last year's weak areas rather than repeating the whole syllabus blindly.
  • Personal error-log notebook reviewed weekly by faculty
  • NCERT line-by-line reading sessions for Biology
  • Fortnightly full syllabus mocks from November onward
Foundation, Class 8 and 9
Weekend-only batches that stay ahead of school by two chapters and build olympiad-level problem solving without pressure.
  • Mental maths drills, 10 minutes at the start of every class
  • NTSE and olympiad question exposure from day one
  • No homework beyond one practice sheet per week, by design
