The build sequence

Five stages. No surprises at stage four.

A building is a sequence, so we run it as one. Each stage closes with a signed checklist before the next mobilises. You always know which stage your money is standing in.

01

Survey, soil and sanction

Weeks 1 to 6

Contour survey, geotechnical report, and drawings taken through sanction. We do not price what we have not measured.

02

Design freeze and BOQ

Weeks 6 to 10

Item-rate BOQ against frozen GFC drawings. Every rate is traceable, every deviation goes through a written change order.

03

Structure

Month 3 onward

Footings to terrace slab with pour cards, cube tests and a weekly photo log sent to your phone. Slab cycles published in advance.

04

MEP and finishes

Overlapped with structure

Conduiting, plumbing pressure tests, then finishes in one direction, top floor down. A mock-up flat is approved before bulk work begins.

05

Snag list and handover

Final 30 days

Joint inspection, a numbered snag list, closure within 21 days, then keys, warranties, as-built drawings and the completion file.
