Safety and warranty

The boring paperwork that protects you

A dedicated EHS officer on every active site, toolbox talks each morning, and a warranty file you receive at handover, not a promise you chase later.

What does the structural warranty cover?

10 years on the structural frame and waterproofing, 1 year comprehensive on finishes and MEP. Terms are printed in the handover file with named contacts for warranty calls.

How do you control material quality?

Steel and cement only from approved makes listed in the contract. Concrete cubes tested at a NABL-accredited lab, results filed against each pour card.

What happens if the schedule slips?

The weekly lookahead flags slippage early. If a delay is ours, liquidated damages apply as written in the agreement. No renegotiation at stage four.

Are your sites RERA compliant?

Yes. Registration numbers, quarterly progress uploads and escrow discipline are handled by our compliance desk and shared with buyers on request.
