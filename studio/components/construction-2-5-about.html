Section 03

Method statement, in plain terms

Four controls run on every site, every week. Ask any of our clients which one saved them money.

QC-01 . MEASURE FIRST

Digital survey before pricing

Total-station survey and soil report precede the estimate, so the BOQ prices what exists, not what the sketch hoped.

QC-02 . FREEZE THE SET

GFC drawings locked before mobilisation

Changes after freeze go through numbered revision clouds and a signed change order. Rev C means two changes, both priced.

QC-03 . HOLD POINTS

No pour without a signed checklist

Rebar, cover, shutter line and level are inspected against the drawing at defined hold points. The checklist is photographed and filed.

QC-04 . CLOSE THE LOOP

As-built set at handover

You receive red-line as-builts, test certificates and warranty cards as one indexed file, within 15 days of handover.
