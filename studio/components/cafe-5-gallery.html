The roastery

Roasted eleven meters from where you drink it.

Our 12 kilo Probat sits behind the counter, not in a warehouse. We roast every Tuesday, rest the beans five days, and pull the first shots the following Sunday. If a batch tastes flat, it goes to compost, not to your cup.

The bakery

Bread that kept us up all night, so it never keeps you waiting.

The levain is older than the shop. Every loaf proofs for 36 hours in the cold room, gets scored by hand at 4 a.m., and lands on the shelf with the crust still ticking as it cools. Saturday is rye day. Set an alarm.
