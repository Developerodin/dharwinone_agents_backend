Questions we hear at the counter

Do you take reservations?

No, and we like it that way. Seats turn over fast in the morning. Groups of six or more can email us for the long table by the window after 11.

Can I buy your beans?

Yes. Whole bean 250g bags of whatever is on the grinder, roasted that week. We will grind for your brewer if you tell us what you use at home.

Is there decaf, and is it actually good?

A sugarcane process Colombian that regulars order without whispering. We pull it on its own grinder so it never tastes like an apology.

Do you do wholesale for other cafes?

A small list, capped so quality holds. Ask for Jonah on a Tuesday and bring your favorite cup.
