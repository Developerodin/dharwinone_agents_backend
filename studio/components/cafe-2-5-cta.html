The ritual

Three rules of the night shift.

We opened because the city's best conversations start after the other cafes stack their chairs. Everything here is tuned for the late hour: dimmer lights, quieter grinders, longer welcomes.

I

No wifi after ten

Laptops sleep, people talk. The router does too.

II

Decaf with dignity

Sugarcane process Colombian on its own grinder, pulled with the same care.

III

Last pour at 12:40

Enough time to finish it slowly. We will never rush your cup.
