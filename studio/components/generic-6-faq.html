Common questions

Answer the questions you hear every week

Every answered question here is one less hesitant phone call, and one more confident one.

How much does it cost?

Give a starting price or a typical range, even a rough one. Pages that name a number get more enquiries than pages that hide it. Add what changes the price so quotes feel fair, not arbitrary.

How soon can you start?

State your honest lead time. "Usually within a week, urgent jobs sooner" beats silence. If you keep an emergency slot, say so here.

Which areas do you cover?

Name the localities you actually serve. Being specific helps the right people find you and stops the wrong calls before they happen.

What if something goes wrong?

Describe your make-it-right policy in plain words: what you fix, how fast, and at whose cost. This single answer wins more trust than any award badge.
