How we teach

A term at {{BRAND}}, step by step

Nothing here is a secret method. It is the ordinary things, done every single fortnight without fail.

01

Diagnostic test, week one

Before teaching a single chapter, we test what the student already knows. The study plan is adjusted to close their specific gaps, not the average student's.

02

Fortnightly progress reports

Every two weeks, parents receive a one-page report: chapters covered, test scores, attendance, and one line from the class teacher. Signed, on paper.

03

Saturday doubt hour

An open hour where any student can bring any question, from this week's homework to last year's board paper. Faculty sit with students one on one.

04

Full-length mock series

Six mocks on the actual board and entrance patterns, held on Sunday mornings under exam conditions, with answer scripts returned and discussed within 3 days.
