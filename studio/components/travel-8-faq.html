Before you pack

The questions everyone asks on the first call.

How fit do I need to be?

Every route has an honest grading on its day sheet. If a day says demanding, it means 8 hours and real elevation. We will talk it through before you pay a deposit, and we have turned people toward easier routes plenty of times.

What exactly is included in the price?

Everything on the day sheet: guides, park fees, transfers, lodging, and every listed meal. Not included: international flights, tips, and the bottle of wine you will insist on buying Elena.

Can I book a private departure?

Yes, any route can run privately for groups of 4 or more. Families do this often for the Kyoto and Zanzibar routes. Pricing lands about 15 percent above the group rate.

What happens if weather closes a trail?

The guide swaps in the alternate day, which is planned in advance for every single route. In nine years we have refunded two trips and rerouted hundreds. Nobody sits in a hotel lobby.
