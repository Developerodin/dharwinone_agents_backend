What we do

Three things, done well

Service one

Your main offering

Describe the service most customers come to you for. Say who it is for, what they walk away with, and how long it usually takes. Specifics beat adjectives: a timeline, a price range, a guarantee.

Example shape: "Full kitchen deep clean, two cleaners, done in under three hours, satisfaction rechecked before we leave."

Service two

Your second strength

The thing customers discover once they trust you. Explain how it connects to the first service, and why buying both together works out better for them.

If you only do one thing, delete this row. A short honest list beats a padded one.

Service three

The specialist request

The unusual job you handle that most others turn away. This row often wins the customers who searched the longest, so be precise about what qualifies.

State plainly what you do not do. Saying no to the wrong work reads as confidence.
