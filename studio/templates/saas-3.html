What the platform holds

Four registers, one authoritative record

01

Contract register

Every agreement with its owner, renewal date, notice period, and negotiated terms. Renewal alerts go out at 120, 90, and 30 days, to a person, not a shared inbox.

02

Risk register

Vendor security reviews on a fixed cadence, scored against your own framework. Expired attestations block new spend automatically until reviewed.

03

Spend register

Committed spend against actual invoices, per vendor and per cost centre, reconciled nightly from your ERP. Variances above threshold open a task, not a surprise.

04

Decision register

Who approved what, when, and on what evidence. Every approval carries its supporting documents, so an audit request is an export, not an archaeology project.
