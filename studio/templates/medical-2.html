Who we see

From first vaccines to golden years

The same three doctors, so your file and your history stay in one place for decades, not one visit.

Infants · birth to 2 years

Little ones

Gentle, unhurried visits with a separate well-baby waiting corner so newborns never share a bench with flu season.

A typical visit covers

  • Growth tracking on WHO charts, explained plainly
  • Immunisations on the national schedule, with reminders
  • Feeding, sleep and teething guidance for parents
Kids and teens · 2 to 17

Growing up

School-age checkups that respect a teenager's privacy and a parent's questions, in the right order.

A typical visit covers

  • Annual school health checks and fitness certificates
  • Allergies, asthma and recurring infections
  • Nutrition, screen habits and adolescent wellbeing
Adults · 18 to 59

The busy years

Working-age care built around your calendar, with evening slots and reports on your phone by the next morning.

A typical visit covers

  • Annual health screens: sugar, lipids, thyroid, BP
  • Diabetes and hypertension management plans
  • Women's health, travel vaccines and minor procedures
Seniors · 60 and beyond

Elder care

Longer appointment slots, a ground-floor consulting room, and one doctor who reads the whole medicine list together.

A typical visit covers

  • Medication review to cut duplicate prescriptions
  • Joint pain, memory and fall-risk assessment
  • Home visits within 3 km for housebound patients

Your visit

Small things that make visits easier

● On-time promise

If we run more than 20 minutes late, your consultation fee is waived. It has happened four times this year.

● Reports by morning

In-house lab for the common panels. Samples before noon, results on WhatsApp and the portal by 9 am next day.

● Fair, printed prices

Consultation, vaccine and lab prices printed at reception. Follow-ups within 7 days are free.

● Insurance friendly

Cashless OPD with the four major insurers, and itemised bills your employer's plan will accept the first time.

New to the neighbourhood? First visit is half price.

Bring your old prescriptions and reports. We will set up one family file, and from then on you only ever tell your story once.
