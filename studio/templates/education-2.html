A note on aid

Admission is need-blind. Last year, 61 of 144 entering students received scholarships covering half fees or more, and no offer was withdrawn for want of funds. The bursar's office publishes its criteria in full inside the prospectus.

Applicants writing from smaller towns may sit the entrance test in their own school under proctorship. Write to the registrar before 15 January.
