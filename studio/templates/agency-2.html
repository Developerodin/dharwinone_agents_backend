Method

Four rules before we take an engagement

A number must be at stake

If the problem cannot be stated as a metric someone is accountable for, we decline. Vague mandates produce vague invoices.

We measure before we touch anything

Two weeks of baseline instrumentation comes first, every time. Otherwise the after has nothing honest to be compared with.

Your team does the work with us

The result gets written down, either way

No. 39 / 2025  

Fulfilment backlog at a specialty grocer

E-commerce · 240 staff

Problem as stated

Online orders promised in 2 days were shipping in 6.5 on average. Refund requests tied to lateness had reached 11 percent of orders and the ops lead was hand-triaging spreadsheets nightly.

Intervention

Nine weeks. Rebuilt pick-path logic around actual aisle telemetry, cut carrier handoffs from four to two, and gave floor leads a single morning exception queue instead of three reports.

Measured result

6.5 to 2.1 daysmedian order-to-ship, 90 days post-engagement

Lateness refunds fell to 1.8 percent of orders. Measured on the client's own warehouse data, verified by their finance team.

Engagement fee: fixed, agreed before baselineResults memo signed 14 Nov 2025
No. 37 / 2025  

Claims cycle time at a regional insurer

Insurance · 1,100 staff

Problem as stated

Simple motor claims took 23 days to settle against a regulator expectation of 10. Adjusters spent an audited 40 percent of their day requesting documents that were already in the system.

Intervention

Fourteen weeks. Mapped every handoff in 60 sampled claims, removed two approval steps that no policy required, and built a document checklist that fires at first notice of loss instead of day nine.

Measured result

23 to 8 daysmean settlement, simple motor claims, next full quarter

Complaint volume on the claims line dropped 34 percent in the same quarter. No headcount change.

Two consultants on site, Tuesdays and WednesdaysResults memo signed 2 Sep 2025
No. 35 / 2024  

Kitchen throughput for a 12-site restaurant group

Hospitality · 12 sites

Problem as stated

Friday ticket times averaged 31 minutes against a 18-minute target, and two sites were discounting roughly 900 euros a week in apology desserts and comped mains.

Intervention

Seven weeks across three pilot sites. Re-sequenced the pass around dish assembly time rather than order time, moved prep cutoffs earlier by 40 minutes, and retrained expo leads on a two-signal board.

Measured result

31 to 17 minaverage Friday ticket time across pilot sites

Comps fell 72 percent at pilot sites. The group rolled the changes to all 12 sites themselves, which is the outcome we prefer.

Pilot first, rollout owned by the clientResults memo signed 19 Dec 2024
