How a project runs

Eight weeks, four fights, one launch

Week 1

The interrogation

Two days in a room with your founders. No slides allowed. We leave with the one true thing, written as a single sentence you are slightly afraid of.

Weeks 2–4

Three directions

Three genuinely different routes, presented as printed posters on a wall, never as a PDF. You pick one. Picking pieces of all three is the only thing we refuse.

Weeks 5–7

The build

Identity system, templates, site, packaging dielines. Weekly working sessions with the two designers doing the work, because there is no account layer to translate.

Week 8

Launch loud

Everything handed over in working files with a one-page usage sheet, not a 90-page brand bible nobody opens. Then we help you ship the first loud thing.

Contact

Say the scary version out loud.

One email, three sentences: who you are, what you sell, and what you wish people said about you. We reply within two days, honestly.
