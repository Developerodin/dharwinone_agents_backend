Days here

Nothing is scheduled. Everything is ready.

A loose shape of a day, in case you want one. Every item can find you wherever you happen to be sitting.

6:30 am

Swim before the wind

The lagoon is glass until nine. A guide waits by the kayaks for anyone who wants the reef instead.

Midday

Lunch under the almond tree

Whatever the boats brought in at dawn, grilled whole, with limes from the garden. No menu, one question about allergies.

4 pm

The spa pavilion

Two treatment rooms open to the sea breeze. The signature massage uses warmed coconut oil pressed on the island, ninety minutes, no clock in the room.

Sundown

The headland fire

Drinks poured as the light drops, then dinner at whichever table calls to you. On clear nights the staff carry telescopes up the point.
