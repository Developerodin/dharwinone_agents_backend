Our approach

Three quiet rules we never break

We opened in 2019 with three rules on a sheet of paper taped to the door. They are still there.

Small rooms, always

Twelve mats is the ceiling in every class, ever. Your teacher learns your name in the first session and your injuries by the second.

Breath sets the tempo

Sequences are counted in breaths, not beats. If the room breathes slower on a heavy Tuesday, the class slows with it.

Rest is part of the work

Every class ends with a full ten minutes of guided rest. Not two. We watch the door so nobody has to rush their stillness.

The weekly rhythm
