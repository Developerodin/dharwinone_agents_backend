The build sequence

Five stages. No surprises at stage four.

A building is a sequence, so we run it as one. Each stage closes with a signed checklist before the next mobilises. You always know which stage your money is standing in.

01

Survey, soil and sanction

Weeks 1 to 6

Contour survey, geotechnical report, and drawings taken through sanction. We do not price what we have not measured.

02

Design freeze and BOQ

Weeks 6 to 10

Item-rate BOQ against frozen GFC drawings. Every rate is traceable, every deviation goes through a written change order.

03

Structure

Month 3 onward

Footings to terrace slab with pour cards, cube tests and a weekly photo log sent to your phone. Slab cycles published in advance.

04

MEP and finishes

Overlapped with structure

Conduiting, plumbing pressure tests, then finishes in one direction, top floor down. A mock-up flat is approved before bulk work begins.

05

Snag list and handover

Final 30 days

Joint inspection, a numbered snag list, closure within 21 days, then keys, warranties, as-built drawings and the completion file.

Safety and warranty

The boring paperwork that protects you

A dedicated EHS officer on every active site, toolbox talks each morning, and a warranty file you receive at handover, not a promise you chase later.

What does the structural warranty cover?

10 years on the structural frame and waterproofing, 1 year comprehensive on finishes and MEP. Terms are printed in the handover file with named contacts for warranty calls.

How do you control material quality?

Steel and cement only from approved makes listed in the contract. Concrete cubes tested at a NABL-accredited lab, results filed against each pour card.

What happens if the schedule slips?

The weekly lookahead flags slippage early. If a delay is ours, liquidated damages apply as written in the agreement. No renegotiation at stage four.

Are your sites RERA compliant?

Yes. Registration numbers, quarterly progress uploads and escrow discipline are handled by our compliance desk and shared with buyers on request.

Walk a live site before you sign anything.

Bring your drawings, or just the plot papers. We will show you pour logs, snag registers and a handover file from a finished project.
