House standards

What every SKU must survive

Nothing enters the catalog until it passes all four benches. Most prototypes fail twice before batch one.

STD-01

Drop test

Two meters onto concrete, loaded, ten times. No seam failure.

STD-02

Weather

Forty-eight hours of spray. Contents dry, hardware rust free.

STD-03

Abrasion

Twenty thousand cycles on the rub wheel before any material scores a pass.

STD-04

Repair

Every part replaceable with common tools. We publish the diagrams.
