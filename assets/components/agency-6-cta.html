How we work

Three ways to engage. Each one has edges.

No open-ended billing. You choose the shape of the engagement up front, and the engagement letter holds us to it.

Ask which fits your situation
For a single decision

The Diagnostic

Three weeks inside your numbers and your team, ending in a written point of view and a recommended path. Fixed fee, paid once.

Typical duration: 3 weeks. One partner, one analyst.

For a defined outcome

The Project Engagement

Scoped deliverables with dates: the market entry memo, the compliance remediation, the repositioning launch. Milestone-billed against the letter.

Typical duration: 8 to 16 weeks. Senior-led team of two to four.

For continuity

The Standing Retainer

A named partner on call, a monthly working session, and priority turnaround on anything urgent. Reviewed every quarter, cancellable at each review.

Monthly, quarterly reviewed. Capacity is limited to twelve retainers.
