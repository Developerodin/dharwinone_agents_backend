Section 02 / Method

Every job runs the same four steps.

Step 1 / Free

You describe it

One call or one message. We ask the questions that change the price up front, so the quote is real.

Step 2 / Fixed

We quote in writing

A one-page quote with the scope, the price, and the date. It is valid for 30 days and it does not creep.

Step 3 / Scheduled

The work happens

You get a named person, a start time, and a message if anything moves by more than an hour.

Step 4 / Checked

You sign it off

We walk the result against the quote together. You pay when the sheet matches the work, not before.

Section 03 / Assurances

The parts we put in writing.

Replace each line with your real policy. A specific promise with a number in it is worth ten adjectives.

Response

We reply within one working day. State your true response time, then beat it. This is the first promise anyone tests.

Price

The quote is the invoice. If we find something unexpected, work pauses until you approve the change in writing.

Make-good

If it is not right, we return and fix it at our cost. Name the window, 30 days, 12 months, whatever you honestly stand behind.
