Before you ask

First-visit questions

I have never touched a barbell. Which class first?

Iron Hour, any lunch express slot. Tell the coach it is your first session; you will work with the empty bar and a PVC pipe until your positions are safe, then load follows.

What do I need to bring?

Flat shoes, a towel for Engine Room, and water. We supply chalk, belts, wraps, and loaner boxing gloves for your first month of Fundamentals.

Is there really no joining fee?

Really. No joining fee, no cancellation fee, no annual "maintenance" charge. If you stop coming, you stop paying, and we would rather know why.
